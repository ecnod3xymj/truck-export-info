When sourcing a refuse collection vehicles from China, buyers should focus on three key areas: chassis reliability, body construction quality, and after-sales parts availability. For buyers importing a refuse collection vehicles from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.
