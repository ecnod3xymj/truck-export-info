## Field Notes

A Chinese box truck can be very competitive upfront. As a rough reference, a new non-refrigerated box body truck often lands around USD 18,000 to 35,000 depending on chassis brand, horsepower, body size, and export configuration. Refrigerated versions commonly range from USD 28,000 to 55,000, with higher pricing for stronger insulation, better reefer units, and premium chassis options. Used units can be lower, but condition, rust, and service history matter far more than age alone.

The best imports are those matched to local work, not just exported successfully. A box truck for bakery delivery in Riyadh is not the same as one used for flower transport in Nairobi or grocery distribution in Ho Chi Minh City. Buyers who think in terms of route, cargo, maintenance, and total ownership cost usually get the best return from China’s special-vehicle supply chain.
