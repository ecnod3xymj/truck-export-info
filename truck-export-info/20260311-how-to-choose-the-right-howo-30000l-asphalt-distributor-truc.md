## Field Notes

The export process is usually straightforward, but field experience shows that small mistakes cause most delays. A proper order starts with chassis confirmation, tank volume, road standard, and steering side. For buyers needing a RHD asphalt distributor truck for export, the steering layout should be confirmed early, because changing it later can affect chassis lead time and registration documents.

From a total cost of ownership perspective, the cheapest unit is not always the lowest-cost asset. Consider fuel consumption, nozzle durability, burner efficiency, tire quality, and the availability of spare parts. A well-specified truck may cost more upfront, but if it reduces bitumen waste, improves spray uniformity, and spends less time in workshop downtime, it often delivers a better return over the first two to three years.
