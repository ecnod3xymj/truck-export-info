A special truck operates on a standard truck chassis with a power take-off (PTO) driving the specialized equipment: hydraulics raise, lower or drive the working body while the operator controls the cycle from the cab or a panel. Daily checks cover PTO engagement, hydraulic oil level and body mounting bolts.
