**Q: what's tipper truck**

A tipper truck, also known as a dump truck, is a special-purpose vehicle designed to transport loose materials such as sand, gravel, soil, coal, or construction waste. Tipper trucks are widely used in construction, mining, road maintenance, and agriculture.
