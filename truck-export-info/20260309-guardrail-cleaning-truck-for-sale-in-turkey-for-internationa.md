**Q: are empty propane tanks hazardous waste**

Empty propane tanks should not be treated as ordinary trash. In most cases, they are managed as regulated waste or returned for refill/recycling, depending on local regulations and the tank’s condition. If the tank is in good shape, the preferred option is usually to take it to an approved propane supplier, recycling center, or hazardous waste facility.
