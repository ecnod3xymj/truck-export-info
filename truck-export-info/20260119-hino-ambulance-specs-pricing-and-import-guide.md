# Hino Ambulance: Specs, Pricing and Import Guide

> 日野 救护车

The hino ambulance is a purpose-built 医疗通信 vehicle designed for commercial and industrial applications. Export models from Chinese manufacturers typically feature proven chassis options including Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW, ISUZU and MAN, with factory-direct FOB pricing that runs 30-40% below local dealer prices in most destination markets.

When sourcing a hino ambulance from China, buyers should focus on three key areas: chassis reliability, body construction quality, and after-sales parts availability. Chinese export builds pair proven engines (Cummins, Weichai, ISUZU-derived units) with inexpensive spare parts networks in every major African and Asian port city, making them the dominant choice for fleet operators.

For buyers importing a hino ambulance from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

The hino ambulance is typically configured with a power take-off (PTO) system driving the specialized equipment. Engine options range from 180 to 420 hp depending on capacity requirements, with 6-speed to 12-speed manual or AMT gearboxes. Emission standards range from Euro 3 to Euro 6 depending on the destination market's requirements.
