## Field Notes

Common Chinese and international chassis options include **FAW, Dongfeng, Sinotruk, Foton, Isuzu, and Shacman**. For lighter urban jobs, buyers often choose a 4x2 chassis with a gross vehicle weight of 8 to 12 tons. For heavier lifting or power line work, a 6x4 chassis with a 16 to 25 ton GVW is more practical. A typical mounted truck crane for export may offer a boom length of 8 to 16 meters, lifting capacity from 3 tons to 12 tons, and engine power between 130 hp and 280 hp.

Price is another major factor. A small crane truck from China usually starts around **USD 28,000 to 45,000** FOB, while medium-duty models with better chassis and a stronger crane can range from **USD 45,000 to 80,000**. If you need a **crane truck with flatbed**, the price may rise because the body structure must handle both cargo and lifting loads.
