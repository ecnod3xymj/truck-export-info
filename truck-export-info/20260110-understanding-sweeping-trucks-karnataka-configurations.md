**Q: who is responsible for road maintenance**

Responsibility for road maintenance typically depends on the road type and location. In most cases, local municipal, county, or state transportation authorities are responsible for public roads, highways, and major infrastructure. For private roads, the property owner, developer, or an assigned management entity usually handles maintenance. **Q: how to choose new dump truck**

To choose a new dump truck, start with your application: the material you haul, payload, terrain, and daily operating hours. Match the truck’s gross vehicle weight rating, axle configuration, and bed size to your load requirements. For construction or mining use, pay attention to engine power, torque, transmission type, braking system, and suspension durability. Also consider fuel efficiency, maintenance access, parts availability, and dealer support to reduce downtime. If you operate in tight job sites, a smaller wheelbase may be better; for heavy-duty hauling, a larger off-road model may be more suitable.
