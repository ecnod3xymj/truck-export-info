## Field Notes

The ordering process is usually straightforward when handled in the right sequence. First, send the supplier your exact requirements: vehicle type, capacity, application, destination country, and preferred delivery terms. Second, compare quotations from at least two or three suppliers, focusing not only on price but also on steel grade, axle brand, tire brand, and after-sales support.

Third, confirm the technical configuration and ask for a proforma invoice. At this stage, the supplier should provide drawings or a specification sheet for approval. Fourth, pay the deposit, usually 30 percent, so production can begin. For custom orders, production often takes 20 to 45 days, depending on complexity and factory workload. Fifth, request inspection before shipment and confirm the loading method, usually flat rack, bulk cargo, or container shipping for smaller units or parts.
