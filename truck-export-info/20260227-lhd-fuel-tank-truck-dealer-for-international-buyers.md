## Field Notes

When calculating ownership, consider these realities: tire replacement intervals, pump seal wear, brake maintenance, and the local supply of filters and nozzles. A well-specified Chinese fuel tank truck can be a strong value purchase, but only when matched to the route, fuel type, and service environment. For overseas importers, the winning strategy is not buying the cheapest unit; it is buying the truck that stays productive in the country where it will actually work.

Another common issue is matching the wrong tank material to the fuel and local rules. Carbon steel is widely used and cost-effective, but some buyers need aluminum or stainless steel for corrosion resistance, product purity, or regulatory reasons. Buyers also overlook discharge speed and hose length. In remote oilfield service, a weak pump or short hose can turn a simple refueling job into a labor-intensive process.
